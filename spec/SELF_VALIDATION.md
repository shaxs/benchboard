## Candidate.9 authentic execution authority

Candidate.9 remediates the independent candidate.8 scoped review (CHANGES_REQUESTED; ROOT forgeable execution identity, mandatory nine-fact binding, H1 persisted authority, real producer/consumer integration, terminal vocabulary, one shared clock, watchdog terminal record, supervision evidence, eligibility monotonicity, incomplete-supervision marker, submission freeze, private-evaluation gate, terminal timestamp validation, H4 conservative completion, mutation-claim overstatement), preserved verbatim at artifacts/fairness-review-candidate.8.md. Execution identity was descriptive data rather than launch provenance. Candidate.9 therefore makes official authority authentic by construction rather than by assertion.

runner/execution-authority.mjs is the launch boundary. Identity construction is private to it; nothing in its public surface mints an identity. The only composed entry is openOfficialExecution(), which cannot run without a persisted seed, a live ARMED watchdog instance and a fresh heartbeat carrying that instance's nonce, and which persists the authoritative execution record exactly once with the wx flag. Every later official stage calls resolveOfficialExecution(runId) and reads that record from its canonical path; no stage accepts a caller-supplied in-memory substitute. Resolution re-verifies the record against the independently produced watchdog artifacts it names, so holding a record is not sufficient: the real ARMED record and heartbeat must exist and agree on run, team, PID, instance nonce, start, deadline and ceiling.

Honest limit, stated for the reviewer: this is lifecycle authenticity inside the cooperative trust model, not adversarial security. Any local process can compute a SHA-256, so authenticity rests on the persisted record plus the live-watchdog cross-checks, not on hiding arithmetic. Hand-writing owner artifact files is forging owner evidence, which TRUST_MODEL.md places out of scope exactly as it does editing any other artifact. The behavioural property that is enforced is the one the reviewer specified: no official launch means no authoritative persisted execution record, which means no valid official telemetry, evaluation or report.
